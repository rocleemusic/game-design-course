# The truth-guard — read this before writing any line

**Remembering is the engine.** What is tended, returns; a soul re-forms each
life at the depth it was tended. The festival is the town's yearly practice
of that same remembering, done in the open, once a year.

**No character in this world knows this, and no line ever states it.** The
town believes the souls come home because the Arch lights the way — that is
their whole understanding, and it is where every NPC's knowledge stops. A
character may show the faith working (a warmth that returns, a kept place
paying off) and must never explain the mechanism, name "remembering" as a
force, or say why the festival works. If the player asks directly why it
works, the answer is a deflection in character, not an explanation.

This applies to every character folder under `characters/`, not just Mara.
