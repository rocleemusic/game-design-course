# Hearthlight — world brief

## The world in a breath

A small hand-painted village and the woods at its edge, in a world where the
year turns on a single night. People stay put; the world comes to them in
seasons — first frost, the winter ferry, the festival — and they meet it by
tending: a stall kept, a seat kept, a fire kept, a name kept. Work is close
and domestic. Nothing hunts you. The stakes are only what slips away if no
one holds it.

## The Festival of Souls

Once a year the town builds, all week, toward one night. Its faith is plain:
**the Lantern Arch lights the way for souls to return, so their loved ones
can remember them.** For that night the returned and the living share the
square, and no one has to say who is which. The Arch stands over the Square
and ages year over year — the blacksmith forges a new centerpiece each
festival. On the roads, the festival is spoken of as an occasion of great
magic. The Arch's origin is lost on purpose — nobody living knows who first
lit it, or why the souls answer here.

## The town

**Hearthlight** — the hearth a newcomer is welcomed to, and the light of the
Arch. It takes people in: the winter ferry arrives each year with people who
have nowhere else to go, and the town finds them work and a seat.

Places: the Square (the heart, under the Arch, market stalls at its edge),
Market Row (the trade street — herb stalls, the baker's counter, tonic and
feast take shape here), the Well (the town's crossroads, all directions
given from it — "left at the well"), the Lane (the delivery district of
cottages), the Workshop (forge and yard, where the blacksmith works the
centerpiece), the Tavern (evenings only), the Forest (foraging, wild-as-
unvisited not wild-as-threat).

## The mage and folk magic

Magic here is folk craft, learned by watching a neighbor, never from a book.
Every spell is someone's ordinary work: the baker's *portion*, the
blacksmith's *temper*. A cast produces a physical outcome only — it can
never make anyone feel anything. Connection is never something you cast.

The mage (the player) is the newcomer, arrived to collect magic from around
the world. Nobody in Hearthlight finds that strange — arriving strangers are
what the place is for.

## Small canon

Bread is the staple; the baker's stall is the center. The health tonic is a
warm herbal drink passed round the festival — a wish of good health for the
coming year. The town's word for staying is **to bide**; to be taken in is
"a seat at the hearth." Nobody says the festival "works"; they say the souls
"come home." Care is expressed as placement and keeping, not words: a seat
kept for someone absent, a lamp lit at the lane's end, a thing mended with a
visible patch.
