Game-project · HUD wireframe · 2026-08-23

One Bar, Three Tenants

The HUD collapses to a single centered bottom bar. What lives in it depends on what the player is doing: exploring shows the five nav icons, casting swaps in the satchel strip as the spell picker, dialogue swaps in the VN controls. The top-right corner empties out — NavRow.ts's icon language, tooltips and Tab-focus all survive, they just move. Movement is not a bar concern at all: you walk by clicking the screen.

§1b

Day actions — Wait and End the Day

Two more choices sit at the bottom of every screen today — [Wait] and [End the day] — in the same ink text-button row as the traversal buttons §1 just retired. They can't become move regions: neither one is a place on the screen, so there's nothing to draw a dashed box around. They need a home in the bar itself.

Recommendation (frontend-design / ui-ux-pro-max / WCAG 2.2 AA pass): a fourth cluster at the bar's right end, past Settings, behind its own divider — the existing three clusters are all panels (a noun you open); Wait and End Day are verbs that spend a move or close the day, and mixing them into panel tiles risks a misclick that costs the player something real. Wait stays a plain 44px tile (hotkey W) — frequent, low-stakes, same language as the rest of the row. End the Day is a text pill instead of an icon, reusing the cast tenant's Exit cast · Esc shape — reading End day · E — because a one-way commitment shouldn't ride on an icon-only affordance with no persistent caption. A visible label clears WCAG 1.1.1/4.1.2 without depending on a hover tooltip, and End the Day should open a one-step confirm before it commits. Wait needs no confirm.

§2

Casting — the satchel strip takes the bar

Entering cast mode (right-click a receiver, per the cast-flow redesign) swaps the bar's tenant: nav icons out, satchel chips in — the same interactive picker SatchelStrip.ts becomes in that redesign's Option A. Same bar shell, same position, so the player's hand never travels. An Exit-cast pill on the right end is the way back.

§3
