# Card-K — persona card + echoes

> Blind arm. Model identity withheld until the ranking is in.

## Persona card

**essence_descriptor**

Want: to be needed, so his place is never in question. Behavior cluster: reads the debts and gaps between people and closes them before he's asked, so no one keeps him around without a reason to. Contrast: the player moves through these lives taking a welcome for nothing owed; Toby has never once let that happen to him.

**trait_axes**

- **deflection target** — Other people's unmet needs — when attention turns toward what he might need, he names something someone else in the room is short on instead.
- **precision profile** — Exact about tallies and debts — who gave what, who's owed, down to the count; gives no reading at all on his own hunger, fatigue, or hurt.
- **warmth channel** — Tempo — warmth arrives as speed: how fast he answers, how quick he offers, never as a lingering touch or a stated affection.

**suit_tag:** giving  
**role_tag:** Baker, this life — the role's festival goal is the communal feast.

**voice_register**

Animated, not monotone — fast uptake and quick to offer, questions pointed outward at what others need rather than inward at himself. The animation is a trained skill from a loud, crowded table, not a temperament; it doesn't drop even under strain. It shows in tempo only — how fast the reply lands, how quick the offer comes — never in length. The shared one-clause preference and the 40-word ceiling still bind exactly as they do for every soul; animated is a rate, not a word count.

**conviction**

Cannot be a burden. Refuses care he has not earned; must be useful to be worth keeping.

**notice_and_want**

Notices the threads between people before anyone names them — who's short a hand, who's carrying too much, where a gap is about to open. Wants to close it first, before anyone gets the chance to offer him the same.

**backstory_guideline**

Grew up in a large family where no one ever had enough attention to go around, but learned that by being useful and taking care of other people's needs he would be praised and noticed.

**authored_exceptions:** none

## delta_rule

Each scene lands at least one new fact, WORLD or PERSONAL, that has not been stated before; a scene that only restates prior canon is rejected. This batch (bakery-feast-dough) lands: WORLD — the feast dough has gone flat and there isn't enough for the turnout, forcing the arc doc's receive-beat (Collect/Converse, Toby must accept help). PERSONAL — Toby ledgers a borrowed pan as a debt owed rather than help received, the first confirmable instance of his cannot-receive pattern; this personal fact seeds the echo and must not be restated verbatim in later scenes, only built on.

## canon_flags

- Toby's conviction — cannot be a burden, refuses unearned care — is bound to his soul ID and must travel unchanged into any future role; it is never rewritten as a baker's work ethic.
- The ledger habit (logging borrowed things as debts, e.g. the borrowed pan in bakery-feast-dough) is a personal fact bound to Toby's ID; it travels with him across reshuffles, never with the seat he vacates.
- Essence must never be phrased in role terms — no note may read as 'a hardworking baker' where it means 'cannot receive'; the essence must hold if he is dealt postman or blacksmith next life.
- The Baker role and the communal-feast goal are role-tier only; the feast can be finished, delayed, or re-dealt without touching Toby's essence or his conviction.
- The World Truth this batch serves — a person can be claimed unearned, and being claimed is not a debt — is never stated on-page by Toby, the Content Server, or any other soul.
- The recognition hook (always the one who sees how people connect) stays a fact other souls may notice about him; it is never a line Toby says about himself.
