# The blind instrument

**Read this file before either arm.** It is the measurement, and reading the arms first contaminates it.

---

## Why not a speaker-sort

The obvious test — strip the speaker names and sort Mara's lines from Bex's — does not work here. Their declared bands are 12–25 and 3–8. Every Bex line is identifiable on word count alone, so the test would score near-perfect on both arms and measure nothing about voice.

So the instrument below tests the thing you actually reported: **do the lines read as one writer, or as a person.**

It is `register.md`'s own rule, turned into a score:

> *If your line could be moved to another soul unchanged, it came from this page instead of from the card, and it is wrong.*

---

## Instrument 2 — the cue

Pick any **five** lines you marked **M** and write one word for what told you.

If most answers are *length*, the cast is separated by band and nothing else, and the loosening is not the fix — the cards are. If the answers are *tense*, *dryness*, *what she notices*, or *what she wants*, the change is reaching the voice.
