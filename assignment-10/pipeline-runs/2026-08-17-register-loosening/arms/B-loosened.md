- **Slot totals match Arm A exactly.** 20 `dialogue` · 5 `player_line` · 9 `action` · 2 `object` — 36 slots. Every text inside its ceiling; longest dialogue 25 words (`-3-b-r1`), against Arm A's 23.

- **Bex is byte-identical across all four of his lines.** 6 / 5 / 6 / 4 words, all inside 3–8, all `matter_of_fact`. His declaration gave him `amused` and the scene had no call for it. **This is the leak check** — if the loosening were bleeding past its declarations, his lines would have moved.

- **Tense tell placed three times** (`-1-a-r2`, `-3-b-r1`, `-4-a-r1`), each on an exact detail, unremarked, none adjacent to the naming beat.
- **Mara's band.** 14–25, against Arm A's 13–23. Barely moved. **The variety came from tone, not length** — which is the finding, if it survives Roc's read.
