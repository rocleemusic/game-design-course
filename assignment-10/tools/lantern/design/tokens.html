Revision after Roc's notes, 2026-07-29. Palette now draws from the Festival of Souls GDD pitch (cream · forest · sage · dusk · amber · gold), which carries the garden ref's coloring natively. Every state differs on three channels at once — medallion (icon + fill), left band, and border/glow — so no two states rely on reading a small label. Current = gold lantern glow. Approved = forest seal. Edited = dusk pencil + corner fold. Flagged = clay flag + full border. Visited = gold thread. Pending = dashed ring. Soul markers wear the blossom.
