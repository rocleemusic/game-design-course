# Music

**Maps to:** `OptionsScene` Sound → "Music" row (slider, 0-100). **Bus field:**
`PlayerSettings.musicVolume` (add in Stage 0, same pattern as `panTauMs`).

The score under exploration. Fades on its own during a soul's conversation so
lines are never buried — see `gdd/10-audio.md`'s sonic identity note: Hisaishi /
Ghibli-inspired.

**Belongs here:** location themes, festival stings, the conversation-ducking bed.

**Doesn't belong here:** a one-shot chime tied to a specific action (→
`sound-effects.md` or `spell-cast-sounds.md`), ambient loops with no melodic
content (→ `ambience.md`).

**Proposal note:** most music slots are per-location or per-scene-state, not
per-click — expect fewer, longer-lived slots than the other three categories.
