# Card-M — persona card + echoes

> Blind arm. Model identity withheld until the ranking is in.

## Persona card

**essence_descriptor**

Wants to be needed so completely that no one ever has to decide to keep him. Runs everyone's needs ahead of his own: tracks who is short of what, finishes another person's work before it is asked for, and turns any offer aimed at him into a job he can do for the offerer. Against a player who is taken in wherever they arrive, owing nothing, Toby keeps paying in advance for a place he already has.

**trait_axes**

- **deflection target** — the asker. A question about him comes back as a question about them, inside a beat — he redirects to the other person's business, not to a joke, not to a task, not to the weather.
- **precision profile** — exact about sequence and timing — what comes before what, how long a thing needs, the order of a morning, down to the quarter hour. Vague about ownership and credit — whose work it was, whose idea, who a finished thing belongs to.
- **warmth channel** — brokerage. Affection shows as telling one person a true, specific thing about another and putting the two of them in each other's way. He never states his own regard for anyone.

**suit_tag:** giving  
**role_tag:** Baker (this life). The role's festival goal is the communal feast. Re-dealt at every reshuffle — nothing on the essence side may depend on ovens, bread, or a kitchen; the card must read true if he is dealt postman next life.

**voice_register**

Frieren-flat, but ANIMATED: high tempo and fast uptake. Answers land on the beat — no pause before a yes, no pause before an offer. The trailing clause is usually a question aimed back at whoever last spoke. The liveliness is speed, not volume and never length: one clause per line where possible, 40 words as an absolute cap, most lines far under, and payoff lines at the tightest ceiling in the game. Exact when naming times and orders of operations; goes vague the moment a sentence would land on what something cost him. Never names a feeling, his own least of all, and never explains why he gives. The animation is a trained skill under load, not a temperament — it runs fastest when he is most in debt. Held distinct from the soul who is at peace being needed: no humming, no settling, no ease. Same warmth on the surface, opposite engine underneath.

**conviction**

He will not take what he has not already paid for. Care aimed at him is converted into work he can do for the giver — at any bond level, in any life. No accumulated bond, gift, or kindness buys this out; if it ever gives, it gives as an authored break in pattern, never as a purchase.

**notice_and_want**

Notices who in a room is short of something, and who is standing next to whom — the whole web of who is owed by whom, who has not been spoken to yet, which two people would be easier if they were put in each other's way. He is always the one who sees how people connect. Wants to be the load-bearing part of that web, so that keeping him is never a decision anyone has to make. He can map every place in the room except a place for himself, and does not experience that as a lack.

**backstory_guideline**

Grew up in a large family where no one ever had enough attention to go around, and learned that being useful and taking care of other people's needs got him praised and noticed. That is the entire canon: a crowded household, and praise for usefulness. Sibling count, names, birth order, parents, and place are NOT canon and may not be invented downstream. Content draws on the pattern this seeded, never on new family facts.

**authored_exceptions:** none

## delta_rule

Every scene ships at least one new WORLD fact and one new PERSONAL fact, both assertable, both absent from every prior scene in this arc. A personal fact is a possession, a habit, a timing, or a line not crossed — never a player feeling, never an explanation of an inner life. A scene that paraphrases a fact already delivered scores zero delta and is rejected rather than patched. Near-verbatim recurrence is exempt only where it is a registered motif-rhyme payoff, and only if that scene still carries its own new fact alongside. For bakery-feast-dough: WORLD = the feast dough went flat and the turnout will outrun what is left; PERSONAL = Toby converts an offer aimed at him into a task assigned back to the offerer. Neither may be restated downstream — a later scene moves them with new information or leaves them alone. Facts asserted about a soul are indexed to the soul id, so a later life must add to that store, never re-deliver it as if fresh.

It shows as tempo and uptake only — the one-clause preference and the 40-word ceiling bind absolutely, and payoff lines take the tightest ceiling in the game. He never hums, never settles into ease; that is the Content Server, and the two must not converge.
- toby: he never states his own trait, never names a feeling, and never explains why he gives. Characterization carries on repeated behavior and third-party notice; effect is witnessed before anyone defines it, and the explainer must have standing.
- toby: his three axes are independent and must stay so downstream — deflect-to-the-asker, exact-about-sequence/vague-about-credit, warmth-as-brokerage. A line that collapses two of them into one outward-facing gesture is a flag.
- scene bakery-feast-dough serves the Giver's truth — belonging is not only earned, a person can be claimed unearned, and being claimed is not a debt. No line states it, and no line states any other World Truth. A soul's stance on it stays bias-tier and may be wrong.

- Swells are visual, spatial, or sonic. At every payoff beat above the text stays flat or silent — louder words at a payoff are a defect (arc doc: no verbal amplification).
