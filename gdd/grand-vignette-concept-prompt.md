# Concept-image prompts — the Grand festival vignette

Image-generation prompts for concept art of the **Grand** festival vignette — the top tier of festival night, where the magic display reaches full flood and the lights become the souls of the world. Companion to [`09-art-direction.md`](09-art-direction.md) and the Festival-night section of [`00-world-bible.md`](00-world-bible.md).

Written for a general image model. The palette below is folded in from `09-art-direction.md`'s base preamble — keep it locked to those colors so the concepts read as the same world.

## Base prompt *(shared across all framings)*

> Hand-painted concept art, Studio Ghibli watercolor warmth with the quiet restraint of *Frieren* — wonder over spectacle. A cozy village square at night during a festival of the dead. A great weathered stone-and-iron **lantern arch** rises over the square, crowned with a freshly forged metal centerpiece that glows from within. Market stalls, strung paper lanterns, a scattering of townsfolk. Palette: warm woods, cream, honey and amber, antique gold, soft sage and leaf green, deep soft sky tones. Warm lantern light on the ground rising into a luminous cool night sky. Above the crowd, drifting points of light are **becoming translucent human figures** — the returned, softly and gently rendered, glowing, not ghostly or frightening, indistinguishable in warmth from the living below. A feeling of hushed wonder and belonging. Painterly Miyazaki brushwork, soft edges, atmospheric depth, gently saturated, no neon, no harsh photographic detail, no muddy darkness. No text, no UI.

## Framings *(a few distinct concepts)*

**1 — Wide establishing.** The full square from a slightly raised angle. The arch centered, the crowd filling the space, the whole sky above alive with lights-becoming-souls, thickest and brightest directly over the arch — the souls of the world at full flood. Awe held at a distance.

**2 — The arch and the lifting.** A mid shot on the lantern arch and its glowing centerpiece. A handful of figures — living and returned side by side — look up as the lights lift off the lanterns and take shape. Intimate scale, the magic close enough to touch.

**3 — The kept seat (impermanence in one frame).** Foreground: a single empty chair or an unlit lamp, plainly kept for someone. Background, soft focus: the grand display blazing over the square. Wonder and absence in the same image — the brightest night, and one place still held for someone who did not come.

## Notes for the artist

- **Grand is the top tier.** Quiet and Warm show smaller displays (a thin scatter of lights; lights gathering with shapes almost forming). This vignette is the fullest — reserve the souls-of-the-world tableau for Grand only.
- **The swell lives in the picture, not in words.** This is the game's "going big" moment (`09-art-direction.md`, "Going big"). The image carries the wonder; nothing needs to be captioned.
- **Keep it warm, not eerie.** The returned are welcome, not haunting. The line between living and returned should be hard to find.
